Role: Staff / Senior Cloud Platform Engineer
Company: NovaTech Solutions
Location: Remote / San Francisco, CA

About the Role:
NovaTech is looking for a Senior/Staff Cloud Platform Engineer to spearhead our next-generation cloud infrastructure and microservices ecosystem. You will be responsible for scaling our distributed backend systems, hardening our Kubernetes clusters, and driving developer velocity across our global engineering organization.

Key Responsibilities:
- Design, scale, and maintain high-throughput backend services using Python, Go, and PostgreSQL.
- Build and optimize multi-region Kubernetes (EKS) clusters and cloud infrastructure with Terraform on AWS.
- Implement robust CI/CD pipelines, automated testing, and zero-downtime canary deployment strategies.
- Drive observability and reliability initiatives across microservices using Prometheus, Grafana, and OpenTelemetry.
- Collaborate closely with product, security, and data teams to deliver high-availability distributed systems.

Qualifications & Requirements:
- 5+ years of software engineering experience in cloud-native production environments.
- Strong proficiency in Python or Go, with deep understanding of concurrency, data structures, and asynchronous programming.
- Hands-on expertise with AWS cloud services (EKS, EC2, RDS, S3, IAM, CloudWatch).
- Deep experience with container orchestration: Docker, Kubernetes, Helm charts, and service meshes.
- Strong background with relational databases (PostgreSQL) and distributed caching (Redis).
- Proven track record with CI/CD automation (GitHub Actions, ArgoCD) and Infrastructure as Code (Terraform).
- Excellent communication skills and passion for mentoring engineers and writing clean, maintainable documentation.

Nice to Have:
- Experience with Apache Kafka or event-driven streaming architectures.
- Familiarity with SOC 2, ISO 27001, or enterprise cloud security standards.
- AWS or CKA certifications.
